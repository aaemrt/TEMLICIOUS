About Temlicious

Temlicious is a food business or a shop that focuses on the use of tempoyak as an ingredient in every menu.

The durian used to make tempoyak is in very good condition, as are the other ingredients.

The durian used is like local durian, monthong, and musang king.

This shop has a Western Indonesian theme.

We sell Western food as well as Indonesian food, so customers can try various dishes made from tempoyak.

Our food is homemade and cooked daily using the best quality ingredients.
